Creative work, in the real world.

The Whispers of Forgotten is a novel that reimagines real-world scenarios through an imaginative lens. Its protagonist, Grace, is trapped in her subconscious. When she plays her mother's childhood music box, she enters a realm of suppressed emotions, and her childhood self sits in the portraits. The portfolio around it holds memoir pieces, short stories and poems. A friend painted the scenes from my story: Grace, Hope, Death and Guilt.
